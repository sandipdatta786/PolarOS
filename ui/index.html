The Architecture

One append-only event log. Every action (cargo scanned, diesel consumed, party checked in, item packed) is one immutable event.

Three copies offline for months. Goa HQ, ship, and station each keep their own log. When a satellite link appears (even 15 minutes), they sync: "I have events 1–4821, send me everything after."

No conflicts. Ever. Because nobody edits anything, there's nothing to fight about. Merge is just combining the events chronologically.
