How We Know This
The 28-day trailing average consumption is calculated from actual STOCK_CONSUMED events in the database. We divide total consumption in the window by exactly 28 days (not the number of log entries). At 129 L/day with 2,255 L on hand, the math is simple: 2,255 ÷ 129 = 17.5 days from 15 Sep = 2 October.

Why This Architecture Matters

No internet? No problem. Each station keeps its own copy of the log, logging consumption events offline. When a satellite link appears (even for 15 minutes), stations sync: "I have events 1–4821, you have 1–5000, send me 4822–5000."

No clocks needed. Goa doesn't say "your clock is wrong"; each node just numbers its own events. A station that has been offline for 6 months can sync when it finds a link, and there's no conflict because nobody edited anything.

The forecast screen is real. When the officer at Maitri taps "Forecast," they see the forecast that their data would give them right now—not a guess, not a cached screen. The math runs live from the append-only log.

All numbers are from actual season.db queries.
